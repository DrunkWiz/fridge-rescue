## 1. What the app is

Fridge Rescue helps you keep track of the food you have, use it before it goes off, and give long-life surplus to a food bank. It is a **hackathon demo**: it isn't published on any app store, and features may change or stop working.

## 2. Food safety is your call

- Use-by dates in the app come from you, from a product database, or from typical shelf lives for a category. **They are estimates.** Always check the label, and look, smell and use your judgement before eating anything.
- Recipes (built-in or AI-written) are suggestions. Check ingredients for allergies and dietary needs, and cook food safely and thoroughly.
- "Froze it" adds time to a date as a rough guide. Follow proper freezing and thawing practice.
- We are not responsible for illness, allergic reactions or other harm from food you eat, cook or store.

## 3. Donating food

- Drop-off points come from OpenStreetMap, a bundled list, or places you add. Details such as addresses and opening hours may be wrong or out of date. Check with the food bank before you go.
- Each food bank has its own rules about what it accepts. The app's suggestions don't guarantee a donation will be taken.
- "I dropped these off" is on trust; the app doesn't verify donations.
