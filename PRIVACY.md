# Fridge Rescue Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 25 September 2026

Fridge Rescue ("the app") is an open-source food-waste app built for the RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 hackathon by the developer at [github.com/DrunkWiz](https://github.com/DrunkWiz) ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **there are no accounts, and your fridge stays on your phone.**

## What stays on your device

Everything you put into the app is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us:

- the food in your fridge (names, categories, quantities, dates, what you ate, froze, donated or binned)
- your shopping list
- your meal diary, including any photos you take for it (copied into the app's own storage)
- Sprout's name, outfits, seeds, badges, streaks and check-in days
- your settings, including admin mode

We don't run a server that receives this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

## When the app contacts other services

Some features need to talk to a third-party service. Each one only happens when you use that feature.

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Subscriptions (Pro) | [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) | An anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat, device and app details, and your purchase and subscription status. In this demo, purchases run on RevenueCat's Test Store and no money is charged. |
| Optional rewarded ads | [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) | Data Google uses to serve and measure ads, which can include your device's advertising ID. Ads only load when you tap "watch". This demo uses Google's test ad unit. |
| Ad measurement | [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) | Ad events (loaded, shown, clicked, estimated revenue) linked to the same anonymous RevenueCat ID. |
| Barcode lookup | [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy) | The barcode number you scanned. |
| Finding a food bank | [OpenStreetMap Overpass API](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) | Your approximate location (latitude and longitude), only if you allow location access, to search nearby. |
| Typing a town or postcode | [OpenStreetMap Nominatim](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) | The place name you typed. |
| AI recipes and receipt scanning (Pro, when enabled) | [Anthropic (Claude)](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) | The names of the items you picked for a recipe, or the receipt or grocery photo you chose to scan. Nothing else from your fridge. |

These services handle data under their own privacy policies, linked above. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** only when you scan a barcode or receipt, or add a photo to your meal diary.
- **Location:** only when you look for a nearby drop-off point. You can type a place instead.
- **Notifications:** local reminders the evening before food expires, and a weekly recap. They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

## Sharing

When you share your impact card or your shopping list, the app opens your phone's share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is up to you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

- Clear your data at any time by deleting the app (or clearing its storage in your phone's settings).
- Don't want ads? Don't tap "watch". They are always optional, and Pro users never see them.
- To ask RevenueCat about the anonymous purchase data it holds, see its privacy policy linked above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. The full history is in this repository's commit log.
